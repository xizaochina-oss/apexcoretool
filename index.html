4. Technical FAQ & Global Troubleshooting (50 QA)

Q01: Why do diamond saw blades wobble or burn in high ambient temperatures (Middle East/Southeast Asia)?
Solution: High heat causes soft steel cores to expand unevenly. ACT uses laser-welded segments and thermal expansion laser-slots to dissipate heat rapidly. Maintain adequate wet cooling when cutting high-PSI concrete.
Q02: How do I choose between M14 and 1-1/4"-7 UNC threads for European vs North American core drills?
Solution: European machines (Bosch, Hilti) usually adopt M14 threads or 1/2" BSP. North American handheld drills use 5/8"-11, while heavy rigs use 1-1/4"-7 UNC. ACT provides brass and hardened steel thread adapters.
Q03: Why do core drill bits glaze over when drilling ultra-hard granite or 5000+ PSI rebar concrete?
Solution: Overly hard bond matrix prevents diamond grit release. ACT soft-matrix bits continuously expose sharp synthetic diamond particles. To un-glaze a dull bit, drill into abrasive sandstone for 15 seconds.
Q04: What causes premature segment loss during heavy road sawing in highway repair projects?
Solution: High-frequency silver-brazed segments can melt under high temperature. For dry cutting or asphalt road sawing, always specify ACT laser-welded blades.
Q05: Why is my diamond blade chipping fragile ceramic or porcelain tiles on the edges?
Solution: Segmented blades cause excessive vibration. Switch to ACT ultra-thin continuous rim blades with fine grit synthetic diamond matrix.
Q06: What is the optimal RPM speed for 350mm (14 inch) concrete saw blades?
Solution: Recommended operational speed is 2,500 to 3,000 RPM. Exceeding recommended RPM risks core cracking; under-speeding causes segment glazing.
Q07: How to avoid undercut wear when cutting highly abrasive asphalt pavement?
Solution: Abrasive slurry wears away steel core under the segment. ACT road saw blades incorporate tungsten carbide drop-segment protection to shield steel cores.
Q08: Can I use wet diamond core bits for dry drilling inside finished indoor properties?
Solution: Wet bits used dry will overheat rapidly. ACT dry core drill bits feature turbo segments with air-cooling spiral flutes designed for dust-extracted dry drilling.
Q09: What is the difference between Grade A synthetic diamond grit and standard industrial diamond?
Solution: Grade A synthetic diamonds feature single-crystal octahedron geometry offering 40% higher impact strength and longevity under rebar striking.
Q10: Why does the core bit get jammed inside deep concrete walls?
Solution: Inadequate slurry flushing or misaligned drilling rigs cause core jam. Ensure core drill rigs are anchored solidly and water pressure is maintained at 2-3 bar.
Q11: Are ACT arbor sizes compatible with standard 22.23mm (7/8") and 25.4mm (1") power saws?
