Global Technical FAQ (50 Buyer QA)
Q1: Why do diamond saw blades wobble or burn in high ambient temperatures?
Use ACT laser-welded segments with thermal expansion slots to prevent core warping under extreme friction heat.
Q2: How to choose between M14 and 1-1/4"-7 UNC thread fits for North American vs European tools?
M14 is standard for European handheld grinders; 1-1/4"-7 UNC is standard for heavy core drill rigs in North America. ACT provides conversion adapters.
Q3: Why do core drill bits glaze over when drilling hard granite or 5000+ PSI rebar concrete?
A hard bond matrix prevents diamond exposure. Drill into soft sandstone for 10-15 seconds to expose fresh diamond grit.
Q4: What causes segment loss during road cutting in highway construction?
High friction heat melts silver brazing. Specify ACT laser-welded road saw blades for high-stress dry/wet asphalt cutting.
Q5: How to prevent edge chipping on fragile ceramic and porcelain tiles?
Switch from segmented blades to ACT ultra-thin continuous rim or fine mesh turbo blades.
Q6: What is the recommended RPM speed for a 350mm (14 inch) concrete saw blade?
Recommended operating speed is 2,500 to 3,000 RPM for optimal cutting efficiency and core stability.
Q7: How to prevent undercut wear on steel cores when cutting abrasive asphalt?
ACT asphalt blades incorporate tungsten carbide drop-segment protection to shield steel cores from slurry erosion.
Q8: Can wet core drill bits be operated dry indoors?
Wet bits overheat when dry. Use ACT dry core bits equipped with turbo air-cooling flutes.
Q9: What is the advantage of Grade A synthetic diamond grit?
Grade A single-crystal diamonds provide 40% higher impact strength when striking rebar inside concrete.
Q10: Why does my core bit get stuck inside deep concrete wall penetrations?
Inadequate slurry flushing or loose drill stands cause binding. Maintain steady water flow at 2 bar.
