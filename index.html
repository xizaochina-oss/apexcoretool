Science & Selection Guide

5 Core Technical Factors That Decide Performance & Life

1. Diamond Grade & Grit Size

High-grade monocrystalline diamond = superior compressive strength. Coarse grit = faster cut. Fine grit = smoother finish.

2. Bond Matrix Formulation

Copper / Iron / Cobalt based. Rule: Hard material → Soft bond · Soft material → Hard bond.

3. Diamond Concentration

High concentration extends life. Medium concentration keeps self-sharpening and cutting speed balanced.

4. Welding Technology

  • High-Frequency: Cost-effective wet cutting
  • Laser Welding: Dry cutting, extreme bond strength, zero tooth loss

5. Steel Core & Tensioning

Premium 65Mn or 75Cr1 quenched steel. Dynamic balancing ensures no wobble or overheating at high RPM.

Maintenance & Troubleshooting

10 Essential Practices to Maximize Blade Life & Safety

01 Dressing / Sharpening on refractory brick or abrasive sandstone
02 Precise high-pressure water jet on the cutting point – never cut dry mid-way
03 Strictly follow max RPM and check flange flatness
04 Core bit jamming: retract slightly, increase water, then advance slowly
05 Insert wooden wedges in deep cuts to prevent pinching
06 Light pressure, steady feed – never force the blade
07 Arrow direction must match spindle rotation
08 Clean cement slurry after use and apply anti-rust oil
09 Dry cutting: 10-15s work → 2-3s idle cooling
10 Pre-operation check for cracks or weld gaps – discard defective blades
